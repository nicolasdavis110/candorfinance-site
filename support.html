How can we help?

For app issues, bank connection questions, account access, feedback, or feature ideas, email Averlen support.

Contact support

support@candorfinance.app

Please describe the issue and your device and app version if relevant. Do not email passwords, bank credentials, or full financial records.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings. Deleting the Averlen app or account does not cancel billing. Apple's cancellation instructions explain the steps.

Privacy and account deletion

You can request account deletion in Averlen Settings. For questions about your information or privacy rights, email privacy@candorfinance.app. See our Privacy Policy for details.
